# Live demonstration and validation

Start with **New RFx**. Enter the annual 30-item requirement through the chat; do not load fixtures or prefill model results. Confirm quantities, specifications, three delivery hubs, contract dates and qualification criteria. Answer buyer clarifications before reviewing vendors.

## Before recording

1. Configure the Azure connection privately. Never display or record the key.
2. Confirm delivery within 14 calendar days from PO issuance, payment within 30 days from invoice receipt and no advance payment.
3. Review seven selected suppliers, email wording and the attached RFQ PDF. Approve invitations; source replies arrive automatically and NorthStar remains pending.
4. Extract each received package through the model. Inspect prices, quantities, currencies, units, availability and evidence in the quote summaries.
5. Exclude Metro only after recording the high-price rationale. Resolve Bharat delivery, Jaipur missing toner offers and certificate, Rajan pack sizes/model, and Pioneer Pune coverage/freight/sample approval through targeted supplier clarifications. Extract each revised package.
6. Confirm any USD conversion rate and reference date as an explicit evaluation assumption, not an asserted market rate. Verify four comparable offers before comparing awards.

## The recorded analyst conversation

Open **Compare & Ask**. Show the overview briefly; spend most time asking questions and following the evidence.

1. “Compare the complete quality-cleared single-supplier bids. Explain exclusions, include known freight and show a cost table and chart.” Open the returned cost breakdown.
2. “What is the cheapest per-line split among vendors who cleared the questionnaire? Compare against the best complete single bid.” Check computed coverage, freight and savings.
3. “Does Bharat still qualify for the paper discount under that allocation? What changes if those paper items are split?” Check awarded volume, threshold and discount basis, not a blanket percentage.
4. “Which supplier says it qualifies but has contradictory supporting evidence?” Open Jaipur’s dated certificate record, not its Yes answer alone.
5. “Which lines came from the historical schedule, and what is still unknown?” Open QuickDesk’s current email and actual prior-rate attachment. Unknown freight is not zero, and historical readings require confirmation.
6. Invite the evaluator to choose an unplanned item, subset or exclusion question. Unsupported constraints must receive an honest limitation.

## Revise a source, then ask again

In a supplier quote summary, ask for a specific missing offer, freight amount, current-rate confirmation or corrected delivery commitment. Approve the simulated clarification; inspect the revised originals and re-extract them with the model. Return to the same analytical question. Show that the prior answer is marked out of date and that the current answer recomputes from the changed data.

## Finish with a decision

Open **Award Recommendation**. Review assigned items, costs and practical trade-offs. Missing mandatory delivery/payment commitments must prevent confirmation, even if the model omitted a blocker. Complete the review statements and buyer rationale; confirm the recommendation and download the note/supporting records. This is a recommendation, not a purchase order.

## Deliverables and live-session evidence

- Working prototype accessible under the intended reviewer policy.
- A genuine screen recording of the analyst conversation. A prepared script, synthetic chat or UI-only tour does not satisfy this deliverable.
- The one-page decisions note from docs/DECISIONS.md (PDF supplied separately).
- Source code handover; a GitHub repository remains dependent on an authorized GitHub connection.

Do not silently replace failed model extraction or analysis. Preserve the error and demonstrate a supported recovery. The simulator intentionally supports only recognized clarification topics; it is not an autonomous negotiation model.
